*         DEFS6612 - DEFINITIONS FOR THE 6612 CONSOLE.
*
*         TEXT FOR XTEXT: PROGRAMS CALL IT WITH
*         XTEXT  DEFS6612
*
CHCON     EQU    10B         CONSOLE CHANNEL
LSMALL    EQU    7000B       LEFT SCREEN, SMALL CHARACTERS
LMED      EQU    7001B       LEFT SCREEN, MEDIUM CHARACTERS
LLARGE    EQU    7002B       LEFT SCREEN, LARGE CHARACTERS
LDOT      EQU    7010B       LEFT SCREEN, DOT MODE
RSMALL    EQU    7100B       RIGHT SCREEN, SMALL CHARACTERS
RMED      EQU    7101B       RIGHT SCREEN, MEDIUM CHARACTERS
RLARGE    EQU    7102B       RIGHT SCREEN, LARGE CHARACTERS
RDOT      EQU    7110B       RIGHT SCREEN, DOT MODE
KEYBD     EQU    7020B       KEYBOARD INPUT
COORX     EQU    6000B       X COORDINATE WORD: COORX+X
COORY     EQU    7000B       Y COORDINATE WORD: COORY+Y
*
*         SHOW FN,ADDR,N: SENDS N WORDS FROM ADDR TO SCREEN FN.
*
SHOW      MACRO  FN,ADDR,N
          FNC    FN,CHCON
          ACN    CHCON
          LDC    N
          OAM    ADDR,CHCON
          DCN    CHCON
          ENDM
